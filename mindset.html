Ernest Holmes & The Science of Mind

Ernest Holmes was the founder of the Science of Mind philosophy. His teachings focus on the idea that thoughts influence reality, and that by changing your mindset, you can shift your life experience in a positive direction.

Daily Quotes for Inspiration

  • “Change your thinking, change your life.”
  • “What you believe, you become.”
  • “Your mind is powerful, use it with intention.”
  • “Focus on what you want, not what you fear.”
  • “Everything begins with a thought.”

This page focuses on mindset, inspiration, and daily reminders to stay aligned with your goals. Small thoughts can create big changes over time.

Read The Science of Mind by Ernest Holmes to dive deeper into the philosophy of manifestation and mindset.
